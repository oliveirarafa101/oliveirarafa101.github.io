HOW I APPROACH ENGINEERING

Understand the workflow.
Then connect the pieces.

  1. Start with the people

    Understand who uses the system, the decisions they make and where the process breaks down.

  2. Make responsibilities explicit

    Separate business rules, identity, data and integrations through clear interfaces and contracts.

  3. Follow it into operation

    Consider error handling, human review and maintenance alongside the initial implementation.
